## 2 — LINKEDIN (professional, founder-to-founder)
Most AI is rented. Your data transits a third party, your assistant lives in
someone else's datacenter, and your leverage evaporates the moment the
subscription lapses.

I took the opposite bet: a sovereign agent that runs on commodity hardware.
No GPU cluster, no cloud egress — just a cognition layer on top of an execution
layer, wired into a loop that maintains itself. The result is an autopoietic
system: it regenerates broken parts from what's left, rather than merely
reporting that they broke.

Scarcity was the teacher. On meager compute you architect for regeneration
instead of throughput — and over a year, that curve quietly wins.

I wrote up what it actually takes, including the discipline of NOT over-cooking
an emergent behaviour once it survives the constraint:

https://mandelbroccoli-digital.github.io/blog/2026-08-24-autopoietic-meager-compute.html

If you're building on the local-first side — or weighing why "your data stays
yours" is an architecture and not a slogan — I'd like to hear how.
